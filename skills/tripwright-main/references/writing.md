# Writing rules in full

Read this from `SKILL.md` for a long or English answer. These rules apply to the answer; copy server transport sentences unchanged even when longer.

简明写法：一句一事，动作句用坐、换、走、下车、买、出示、搜开头；条件放在动作前；能核实的写数字，没有的写没核；同一概念一个词。地名单独一行原文，下一行中文名与是什么、链接。用户用什么语言就用什么语言回答；服务端交通原句保留，必要翻译另列并保持同样的范围与不确定程度。第一段不用存的否定句推断，第二段照抄 `sentences`；“怎么查、怎么问”永远在回答末尾，已知稳定事实不推给用户再查。

English answers: before a lookup, send turn's `first_part` as it is, translated if needed (it answers each of the question's `points`); without it, a latest/last-service/can-I-make-it question opens with the stored departure as archived and unchecked and says the real latest follows in part 2; of the hard nodes give only the next one to be at. Other questions answer directly. Once checked, part 2's first body line gives the latest departure found, followed by differences. Line 2 gives "As of HH:MM" from `turn.now` or `date` and necessary assumptions. Use active voice and one fact per sentence; start steps with take, change, walk, get off, buy, show or search. Aim for 20 words per step and 25 per explanation; keep server sentences intact. Each place name keeps its local spelling on its own line, followed by its English name and what it is when the local name is not English (Wien Hauptbahnhof: Vienna's main station). Copy `sentences`, including transfers and limits; do not invent a direct ride or conclude that no later service exists. Copy the transport `note` once per section (timetable, not live), never on every departure; keep explicit live markings. For latest/last-service questions use `latest_departure` with origin and destination only and open part 2 with its `first_line`; use transit `until` for remaining services, through 03:00 the next day for tonight. Archived hard-node text is quoted only as “计划里写过…（存档，没核）”. For latest_departure, `by` means arrival at destination; to catch a service, use `board_at` with the boarding station as destination. Check stable facts yourself. Put `how_to_check.text` and its link only in the final "How to check, how to ask" section, with the exact term to type into the named app, in its original spelling. The user must not need the link. Look up departures only under the triggers. Offer a draft only when asking a person is needed; draft on request, in English and the local language, with other languages when requested. Describe accepted plan changes and their reasons from `changelog`; a rejected edit is "This change did not go through". Keep internal field names and write states out of the answer.
